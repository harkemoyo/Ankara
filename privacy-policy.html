Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Home › Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

At Mary Humphrey Wear, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. We collect information when you create an account, browse our boutique collections, place an order, or subscribe to our newsletter. This includes:

  • Contact Information: Your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address.
  • Order & Measurement Details: Item selections, custom measurements for tailored fits, order history, and delivery instructions.
  • Payment Information: Transaction references and M-Pesa / payment confirmation details (we do not store sensitive payment credentials on our servers).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process, tailor, and deliver your orders smoothly across Kenya and worldwide.
  • Send order confirmations, real-time dispatch updates, and tracking links.
  • Provide personalized customer care via WhatsApp, phone, or email.
  • Send occasional collection drops, style guides, and exclusive offers if you opted in.

3. Data Security & Confidentiality

Your security is paramount. All customer communications and profile data are encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS encryption. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Third-Party Integrations

We work with trusted partners (such as Supabase for secure data management and Google Analytics for store performance) solely to deliver our services. These partners only access information required to perform their functions and adhere to strict confidentiality standards.

5. Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your personal data at any time. You can manage your details in your Account portal or reach out to our team directly.
