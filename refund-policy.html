Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Home › Refund Policy

1. Our Guarantee & Returns Overview

At Mary Humphrey Wear, every garment is handcrafted with care using authentic African fabrics and tailored to perfection. We want you to love your pieces. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, we offer returns and size exchanges within 14 days of receiving your delivery.

2. Eligibility for Returns & Exchanges

To qualify for a return or exchange:

  • The item must be unused, unworn, unwashed, and in the same pristine condition as received.
  • All original tags, labels, and packaging must be attached.
  • Custom-tailored pieces made to specific non-standard body measurements can be adjusted or refitted rather than returned.

3. How to Initiate a Return or Exchange

Starting a return or exchange is easy:

  • Contact our customer care team via WhatsApp at +254 715 687 280 or email maryhumphreyafricanwear@gmail.com.
  • Provide your order number (e.g. #MHAW-XXXX) and the reason for return/exchange.
  • Drop off the package at our Noir Boutique (Muthangari Drive) or Lavington (Kingara Road) stores, or send it via courier to our Nairobi workshop.

4. Refunds & Processing Timelines

Once your returned item is inspected and approved:

  • M-Pesa Refunds: Processed within 24–48 hours directly to the original paying phone number.
  • Card / Bank Refunds: Processed within 3–5 business days depending on your bank's clearance policy.
  • Store Credit / Exchange: Issued immediately upon receipt so you can pick your replacement piece right away.

5. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a defective, damaged, or incorrect item, please notify us within 48 hours of delivery. We will arrange a complimentary replacement or full refund immediately, including all courier shipping costs.

6. Need Help?

We are always here to assist you:

  • Email: maryhumphreyafricanwear@gmail.com
  • WhatsApp / Call: +254 715 687 280
  • Visit Our Stores: Noir Boutique, Muthangari Drive & Lavington, Kingara Road, Nairobi, Kenya
