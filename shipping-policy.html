1. Processing Times

All ready-to-wear orders are processed and dispatched within 1-2 business days. For bespoke and custom-tailored pieces, processing time depends on the complexity of the design, usually ranging from 5 to 10 business days. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will begin processing on the following business day.

2. Delivery Area

We are currently delivering within Nairobi County Only using our designated local riders. Delivery within Nairobi is usually same-day or next-day after dispatch.

3. Outside Nairobi & International Orders

For orders outside Nairobi County or international shipments, please contact us directly via WhatsApp before placing your order. We can arrange specialized courier delivery on a case-by-case basis.

4. Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, you will be notified via email or WhatsApp.
